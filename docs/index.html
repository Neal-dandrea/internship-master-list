About this list

There are two match scores, each from 0 to 100, and both compare a posting with the resumes of the person who maintains this list, so they are personal to them. The keyword score (K) counts how much of what the posting names is on a resume, with rare skills counting for more. The meaning score (M) uses an open-source language model to compare what the posting's sentences say with what the resume's statements say, so it can match two things that are worded differently. The large number is the mean of the two unless you choose one. A dashed score was worked out from the title alone. The label under it names the resume that fit best.

Tags such as "Asks 3+ yrs", "GPA 3.5+", "US citizen or US person", "Clearance" and "Will not sponsor" are read from the posting's own wording by pattern matching. Tags for PhD, MS and the field are guesses made from the title and from what each board reports. Any of them can be wrong, so read the posting.

A date is the day a source says the role was posted, or the day it was first seen here when no source gives one.

Boards read: SimplifyJobs, vanshb03, speedyapply, zapplyjobs, TanhJK728, the PhD Intern Board by dion-jy, Northwestern Fintech, TrakkerHQ and OpenQuant, plus about a thousand company career sites. Credit for curation belongs to the boards. Download the full list as CSV.
