The match score, from 0 to 100, compares what a posting's description asks for with the resumes of the person who maintains this list, so it is personal to them. Rare and specific skills count for more than common ones. A dashed score was worked out from the title alone, because the description could not be read. The label under a score names the resume that fit best.

Listings are gathered automatically from public job boards and company career sites, then merged. Tags such as PhD, MS and the field are guesses made from the title and from what each board reports, so read the posting before relying on them. A date is the day a source says the role was posted, or the day it was first seen here when no source gives one.
